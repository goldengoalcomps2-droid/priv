# Outreach & Cold Message Templates

Use these templates across Instagram DMs, Facebook messages, email, and LinkedIn. Personalise the [BRACKETS] before sending.

---

## INSTAGRAM DM TEMPLATES

---

### 1. To Local Businesses (Web Design / Social Media / SEO)

**First message (warm opener):**
```
Hey [NAME]! I came across your page and love what you're doing with [BUSINESS NAME]. Quick question - are you happy with your current website / social media setup? We help businesses like yours get more customers online. Would love to chat if you're open to it!
```

**Follow-up if no reply (3 days later):**
```
Hey [NAME], just bumping this up! No pressure at all - I noticed a few quick wins that could help [BUSINESS NAME] get more visibility online. Happy to share for free, no strings attached. Let me know!
```

### 10. Cold Email to Small Businesses (Multi-Service)

**Subject:** Quick idea to help [BUSINESS NAME] get more customers

```
Hi [NAME],

I came across [BUSINESS NAME] and really liked what I saw. I noticed a couple of opportunities that could help you attract more customers online:

[Pick 1-2 specific things, e.g.:]
- Your website isn't mobile-friendly, which means you're likely losing 60%+ of visitors
- You're not ranking on Google for "[their main keyword]" - we can fix that
- Your social media hasn't been updated in [X weeks] - consistency drives results

We specialise in helping small businesses grow through web design, SEO, and social media management. Our clients typically see results within the first 30 days.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week? No pressure, no hard sell - just ideas.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
Elevate Services
hello@elevateservices.co
```

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Send initial DM/email |
| Day 3 | Follow up if no reply (see template above) |
| Day 7 | Send a value-add message (free tip, insight, or compliment) |
| Day 14 | Final follow up: "No worries if it's not the right time - just let me know if I can ever help!" |

## PARTNERSHIP OUTREACH

### To Freelancers You Want to Hire (Subcontractors)

**For cleaners:**
```
Hi [NAME], I run a cleaning company and I'm looking for reliable, experienced cleaners to join our team on a subcontractor basis. We provide the clients and handle all bookings - you just turn up and do what you do best. We pay [RATE] per clean and aim to keep you busy with regular work. Interested?
```

**For web designers/developers (overseas):**
```
Hi [NAME], I run a digital agency and I'm looking for a skilled web designer/developer to partner with on client projects. We handle sales, client communication, and project management - you handle the build. Budget per project is typically $300-$800 depending on scope. Regular work available. Interested in discussing?
```

**For video editors (overseas):**
```
Hi [NAME], I'm looking for a talented video editor to handle client projects on an ongoing basis. We provide the briefs and raw footage, you deliver the edits. Typical budget: $50-$150 per video depending on length and complexity. Consistent volume available. Send me your portfolio and rates?
```
