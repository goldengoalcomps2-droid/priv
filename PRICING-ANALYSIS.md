## RECOMMENDED PRIORITY ORDER

1. **Web Design** - Highest margins ($1,100+ per job), sell 3 per month
2. **SEO** - Recurring revenue, builds to passive income, sell 5 retainers
3. **Social Media** - Easy to bundle with SEO/web, sell 5 retainers
4. **End of Tenancy Cleaning** - High demand, partner with estate agents
5. **Home Cleaning** - Volume game, build regular weekly clients
6. **Video Editing** - Easy to outsource, target YouTubers/creators
7. **Dog Grooming** - Repeat customers, emotional purchase
8. **Graphic Design** - Bundle with web design projects
9. **Event Photography** - Seasonal but high ticket
10. **Mobile Massage** - Lower margins but easy to deliver
11. **Cat Grooming** - Niche, less competition
12. **Pressure Washing** - Seasonal peaks (spring/summer)
