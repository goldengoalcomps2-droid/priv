# US Contact Info Setup

## US Phone Number (Receive SMS/Texts)

### Recommended: Google Voice (Free)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Features:** Unlimited US/Canada calls & texts, voicemail transcription, call forwarding
- **Limitation:** Requires a US-based Google account to set up (can use VPN if needed)
- **Sign up:** https://voice.google.com

### Budget Alternative: Fanytel ($0.99/mo)
- **Cost:** $0.99/month
- **Features:** Free incoming calls & SMS, voicemail, no contracts
- **Platforms:** iOS & Android
- **Sign up:** https://fanytel.com

### Budget Alternative: TextNow (Free with ads)
- **Cost:** Free (ad-supported), paid plans ~$10/mo to remove ads & lock number
- **Features:** Calls, texts, voicemail
- **Sign up:** https://www.textnow.com

## US Mailing Address (Receive Mail & Packages)

### Best Value: Anytime Mailbox (from $4.99/mo)
- **Cost:** From $4.99/mo (avg ~$9.99/mo)
- **Features:** Real US street address, mail scanning, package receiving, mail forwarding worldwide
- **Locations:** 2,500+ across the US
- **Sign up:** https://www.anytimemailbox.com

### Runner-Up: iPostal1 (from $9.99/mo)
- **Cost:** From $9.99/mo
- **Features:** Real street address, 4,250+ locations, discounted international forwarding (up to 80% savings)
- **Sign up:** https://ipostal1.com

### Premium: US Global Mail (from $19.95/mo)
- **Cost:** From $19.95/mo
- **Features:** Mail consolidation, worldwide shipping, check depositing
- **Best for:** Higher mail volume, frequent international forwarding
- **Sign up:** https://www.usglobalmail.com

---

## Setup Requirements

### For Virtual Mailbox (all providers)
- Complete **USPS Form 1583** (notarized)
- Two forms of ID (passport + one more)
- Most providers walk you through this online

### For Virtual Phone Number
- Google Voice: US Google account
- Fanytel/TextNow: Email address, app download
