## Creation Technical Stack

### Image Generation
- **Flux (Black Forest Labs)** - Current industry standard for photorealism
- **Stable Diffusion** - Open source, maximum control, free locally
- **Midjourney** - Easiest to use, best for stylised aesthetics ($10-$60/mo)

### Character Consistency (CRITICAL)
- **LoRA Training** - Train on 15-30 images of character face
- 512x512px min, face 60-80% of frame
- Mix: front (40%), three-quarter (30%), profile (20%), expressions (10%)
- Tool: Kohya_ss, rank 16-64, 1000-3000 steps
- Achieves **85-95% facial consistency** vs <10% with prompting alone

### Video (Required for TikTok/Reels)
- **HeyGen** - Avatar talking-head videos, multilingual
- **Kling AI 3.0** - Cinematic text-to-video, realistic faces
- **ElevenLabs** - Ultra-realistic voice generation
