## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Start with 1-2 ideas** - don't spread yourself thin
2. **Build a brand** - simple website, Google Business Profile, social media
3. **Find reliable subcontractors first** - quality controls your reputation
4. **Charge a premium** - position as professional, insured, reliable
5. **Get insured** - public liability insurance is essential (£5-£15/month)
6. **Collect reviews** - Google and Trustpilot reviews are free marketing
7. **Stack recurring revenue** - prioritise businesses with monthly retainers (social media, SEO, bookkeeping, gardening, window cleaning)
8. **Use free/cheap marketing** - Facebook groups, Nextdoor, Bark, Checkatrade, Gumtree
